FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is NEXORA only for builders?

No. The system can be adapted for property dealers, brokers, channel partners, independent agents and real estate teams.

Do I need a CRM already?

No. NEXORA can start with a simple lead CRM and evolve as your business grows.

Can leads come from WhatsApp ads?

Yes. The website is designed around a WhatsApp-first lead journey, while the exact ad setup depends on your Meta account and campaign configuration.

Does AI close the property sale automatically?

No. AI can help with lead capture, qualification, routing and follow-up workflows. Your team remains responsible for the actual sales conversation and closing.

How do I start?

Click any WhatsApp CTA, share your business type and current lead process, and NEXORA can map the first workflow.
